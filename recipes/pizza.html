Homemade Pizza Dough

Description:

This homemade pizza recipe features a simple dough made with flour, yeast, and olive oil, topped with a flavorful tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and your choice of toppings. After kneading and letting the dough rise, bake the pizza at 475°F for 10-12 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly. Perfect for a delicious, customizable meal!

Ingredients

  • 2 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 ¼ tsp active dry yeast (1 packet)
  • 1 cup warm water (about 110°F)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 can (15 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Your choice of toppings (e.g., pepperoni, bell peppers, onions, olives, mushrooms, etc.)

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for about 5-10 minutes until it becomes frothy.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour and salt. Make a well in the center and pour in the yeast mixture and olive oil. Stir until it forms a dough. Knead for 5-7 minutes on a floured surface until smooth and elastic.
  3. Place the dough in a lightly oiled bowl, cover it with a cloth, and let it rise for 1-1.5 hours or until doubled in size.
  4. For the sauce: Heat olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, and basil. Stir for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add crushed tomatoes, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15-20 minutes.
  5. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  6. Once the dough has risen, punch it down and divide it into two portions. Roll each portion into a round shape, stretching it to the desired thickness.
  7. Place the dough onto a pizza stone or baking sheet. Spread a thin layer of sauce, then top with mozzarella cheese and your favorite toppings.
  8. Bake the pizza in the preheated oven for 10-12 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
  9. Let the pizza cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving. Enjoy!
